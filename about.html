About

A gym log built by someone who lifts.

PPL Tracker is an Android workout tracker built by Julien, an independent developer in France who runs a Push Pull Legs program himself.

In one sentence.

PPL Tracker (on Google Play: "Workout Tracker: Gym Log & PPL", in French "Carnet Musculation & Suivi PPL") is a workout log for Android: you record your sets, weights and reps, the app shows your previous session and detects personal records. It works fully offline, with no account, no ads, and collects no data. The log is free; a Pro version is a one-time purchase, with no subscription.

Why this app exists.

I tried a lot of apps to log my workouts. One wanted an account before I could log a single set, another put the rest timer behind a subscription, another showed me an ad between sets. I wanted a fast log that shows what I did last time and asks for nothing else.

I'm a developer, so I built it. PPL Tracker is the app I use every time I train.

Principles that won't change.

  • No ads, ever.
  • No account to create, no email to hand over.
  • No data collected: everything stays on your phone (declared as such in the Google Play "Data safety" section).
  • No subscription: the log is free forever, Pro is a one-time purchase.
  • Your data is yours: back up and export any time, in open formats.

What the app does.

  • Ready-made Push, Pull and Legs sessions, or a free session
  • Log weight × reps in two taps, automatic rest timer
  • Previous session shown next to your current set
  • Automatic personal record detection, deload suggestion when you stall
  • 260+ exercises (barbell, dumbbell, machine, bodyweight)
  • Pro: progress charts, volume per muscle, strength ranking, body weight, CSV export

Android only. Interface in English and French.
