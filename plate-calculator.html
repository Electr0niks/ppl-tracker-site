Barbell plate calculator.

Enter your target weight and instantly see which plates go on each side of the bar. No more mental math between sets.

How to use it.

Pick your gym's bar: a standard Olympic bar weighs 20 kg (45 lb), a women's bar 15 kg, technique or short bars often 10 kg. Untick plates your gym doesn't have: the calculator adapts and tells you when the exact weight can't be reached.

Regular spring clips weigh a few grams and can be ignored. Competition collars weigh 2.5 kg each: tick the box if you use them.

FAQ.

Why can't I hit the exact weight?

Both sides must carry the same load. With 1.25 kg plates as the smallest, the total moves in 2.5 kg steps; without them, in 5 kg steps.

How do I know what weight to aim for?

The 1RM calculator gives you weights for every rep count. Full sessions are in the free PPL program.
