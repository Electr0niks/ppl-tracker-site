FAQ.

Can I bring my history from another app?

There's no automatic import yet. Enter your current weights in your first session and the app remembers them from then on.

What happens when I change phones?

Make a backup from Settings (a free JSON file), send it wherever you like (Drive, email…), and restore it on the new phone. The app can also back up automatically every day. There's no real-time sync: that's the flip side of "zero data sent".
