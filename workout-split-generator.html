FAQ.

My days change every week. What do I do?

Think in order, not weekdays: run the sessions in sequence (Push, Pull, Legs, Push…) whenever you can get to the gym, with at least one rest day every 2 or 3 training days.

How many sessions do I need to progress?

Three well-run sessions are plenty to progress for months. Adding sessions only helps if you recover well (sleep, food).
